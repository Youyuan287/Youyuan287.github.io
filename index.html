Hi! I am Youyuan Tang (唐友源), currently a Ph.D. student in Electronic Information at the School of Robotics and Artificial Intelligence, Hunan University, supervised by Prof. Hui Zhang.

My research focuses on 3D computer vision and intelligent perception, with particular interests in point cloud semantic segmentation, open-vocabulary 3D scene understanding, open-world perception, and embodied perception. I am interested in developing robust and generalizable 3D perception methods for complex real-world environments.

Feel free to contact me for academic discussion and collaboration.
